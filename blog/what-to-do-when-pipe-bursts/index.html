Burst Pipe Emergency Guide in Naperville, IL

Home / Blog / Steps to Take When a Pipe Bursts in Naperville, IL

Few plumbing problems rattle a homeowner like a pipe that suddenly bursts. Water pours out of a burst line at a rate of 4 to 8 gallons a minute, so within hours rooms may be flooded, possessions damaged, and building materials weakened. Knowing exactly what to do in the first critical minutes can save you thousands of dollars in damage and separate a manageable repair from a major restoration project. This guide from Naperville Restoration gives Naperville, IL homeowners a clear action plan for this common emergency.

Step 1: Shut Off the Water Supply Immediately

Your first job after a pipe breaks is simple: get the water shut off as soon as you possibly can. Damage and repair bills grow with each minute the leak runs. Step one is tracking down the valve that controls your main water line. Look by the water meter first, since that is where Naperville houses tend to have it, often in a utility room, garage, or basement, or on a street-side exterior wall. To stop water throughout the house, twist the handle clockwise (righty-tighty) until it stops. Some lines have their own local shutoff, so if you can tell which pipe failed, turn off that valve also.

Your water company can cut the supply at the meter, so call them at once if the valve is missing or will not budge. It is also why every homeowner should learn where the shutoff is long before anything goes wrong. Take a few minutes today to locate yours and make sure it operates smoothly. If it's stuck, have a plumber service it before you need it in an emergency.

Step 3: Limit the Damage to Your Things

Before the crew arrives, you can cut your losses, starting with getting documents, electronics, and valuables out of the wet zone. Lift furniture off wet carpet by placing aluminum foil or plastic blocks under legs. Remove area rugs from wet floors. Mop up accessible standing water with towels. Open closet and cabinet doors to promote air circulation. If you can reach the breaker panel safely and stay out of the water, switch off power to the affected rooms.

Do not step into pooled water anywhere close to outlets, electrical panels, or appliances. If the burst pipe has caused significant flooding and you cannot safely access your electrical panel, leave the electricity on and wait for professionals to assess the situation. Safety always comes first.

Step 4: Record the Damage for Your Claim

Hold off on cleanup until you have a thorough record of the damage to give your insurer. Shoot video and photos showing the standing water, every damaged material, and any belongings that were hit. Note the date and approximate time you discovered the burst pipe, and document the source of the leak if visible. Naperville Restoration supplies detailed reports as well, and paired with your notes they strengthen your claim and your case for fair payment.

How Naperville Homeowners Can Avoid Burst Pipes

Some bursts cannot be avoided, but Naperville homeowners lower the odds a great deal when they insulate exposed pipes in unheated spots such as attics, crawl spaces, and garages. A slight drip from your faucets in a cold snap keeps the water in motion. Warm room air should be able to reach pipes on exterior walls, so open the cabinets under those sinks. Monitor your water pressure and install a pressure reducing valve if pressure exceeds 80 PSI. Braided stainless steel should take the place of worn supply lines, rubber washing machine hoses above all. Above all, make sure you can find your main shutoff long before an emergency.
